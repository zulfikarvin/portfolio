About

Business meets technology — that's where I live.

I am a Master's student at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, currently writing my thesis on multihoming of AI chatbots among Information Technology professionals. My academic journey started at Telkom University in Indonesia, where I graduated Cum Laude with a Bachelor's degree in Information Systems and was recognised as the Most Outstanding Graduate of my faculty.

Across my university years, I competed in 40+ national and international competitions spanning business and technology, served as Chairman of GenBI Telkom University — a 100-member scholarship organisation under the Central Bank of Indonesia — and founded Sieca FRI, the School of Industrial Engineering Competitions Academy.

On the professional side, I interned at PT Len Industri mapping end-to-end warehousing workflows, and worked on real business cases in process mining for Heineken and AI-based solutions for MarketResponse. I hold certifications in IT Governance (COBIT 2019), Cybersecurity Fundamentals (Fortinet), and AI Essentials (Google).

I am passionate about working at the intersection of business and technology to help organisations become more efficient through thoughtful digital solutions. Actively seeking junior roles in the Netherlands.
