About Me

I am a Master's student at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, currently writing my thesis about multihoming of AI chatbots in Information Technology professionals. Previously, I finished my Bachelor's degree in Information Systems from Telkom University, Indonesia, where I graduated Cum Laude as the Most Outstanding Graduate of my faculty. Outside the classroom, I participated in 40+ national and international competitions in Business (Business Plan, Business Case) and Technology (UI/UX, Data Analysis, Hackathons). I also led a 100-member scholarship organization under the Central Bank of Indonesia as Chairman and founded the School of Industrial Engineering Competitions Academy.

On the professional side, I interned at PT Len Industri, a state-owned defence and technology enterprise, where my team mapped end-to-end warehousing workflows using BPMN and conducted process awareness sessions across multiple subsidiaries of the company. I have also solved real business cases on process mining for Heineken and AI-based solution for MarketResponse as part of my Master's programme. I also hold certifications in IT Governance (COBIT 2019), Cybersecurity Fundamentals (Fortinet), and Artificial Intelligence Essentials (Google).

I am based in Amsterdam, graduating in August 2026, and actively seeking junior roles in the Netherlands. I am passionate about working at the intersection of business and technology to help organizations become more efficient through the smart application of digital solutions.

Selected Projects

In Progress

Champio

A learning-and-diagnostics platform for Indonesian university students competing in business case, business plan, and academic essay competitions, currently in development. It combines two modules: track-based learning, with ordered modules per competition format gated by quiz milestones and a library of winning reference papers, and an AI diagnostic engine that lets teams upload a draft and receive rubric-aligned, scored feedback with evidence citations, tracking score improvements across versions.

RoamIo

GitHub Prototype Pitch Deck

An AI travel companion built for digital nomads who are constantly on the move, helping them handle visa-related administrative tasks using information that's currently scattered across the internet and rarely personalised. RoamIo turns what used to take 3 days of research into 3 minutes: users share their situation (nationality, income, destination) and RoamIo generates a personalised plan.

Google Antigravity Claude ChatGPT

DiamondInsights

GitHub Prototype

An AI-powered market intelligence platform designed for a research company whose clients were relying on external AI tools to interpret static research reports, quietly costing the company control over its own client relationships. DiamondInsights maximises the value of those reports through three core features: conversational chat over research documents with chain-of-thought citations for verifiable, exportable insights; personalised cross-selling suggestions based on usage patterns; and a "What-If Engine" that turns historical data into a dynamic, predictive reflection of the market. Placed 4th best project in our Artificial Intelligence course.

Google Antigravity ChatGPT Claude

Buddy Abroad

Presentation

A mobile app helping international students navigate the painful process of settling into a new country, from MVV and student visas to housing, transportation cards, and cultural norms, with information currently fragmented across too many sources. Buddy Abroad brings it together in two features: a Bureaucratic tab with step-by-step, personalised guides and reminders for every administrative task, from applying for a visa to opening a bank account; and an Adaptive tab that turns cultural adaptation into a gamified experience using interactive scenarios and missions tailored to the gap between the user's home country and their new one. I designed the project and used Figma AI to build the prototype. Won 3rd Best Project at the VU x Deloitte Burning Business Quest.

Figma AI UX/UI Design Prototyping

OutfitKu

Video Prototype

Outfitku was born from the environmental toll of fast fashion, with 2.3 million tons of fashion waste generated in Indonesia in 2021 alone, according to the Ministry of Environment and Forestry. Outfitku is an AI-powered personal stylist app that helps users build a wardrobe that reflects their style while staying eco-friendly, through five features: Wardrobe Management to organise and track clothing; Wardrobe Analysis surfacing color palettes, categories, and the utility of existing items; AI-generated outfit combinations based on preference, body shape, and lifestyle; a Community Platform for sharing and swapping outfits to support a circular fashion economy; and Recommendations that encourage more mindful purchases. Won Best No-Code Hack at GarudaHacks, one of Southeast Asia's largest hackathons, where I was project manager and co-designer.
