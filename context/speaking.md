## TEDx Talk — Full Details

Title: "Tailoring Your Own Tapestry"
Event: TEDx Telkom University Main Event
Date: 3rd May 2025

### What the talk is about

The talk centres on how students and young professionals can overcome FOMO (Fear of Missing Out) by shifting their focus from chasing external validation to building sustainable personal growth.

### Key themes

**Living Like a Product**
Instead of joining endless organisations or stacking achievements for appearances, the talk encourages intentionally developing skills and values that align with specific life goals — treating yourself like a product you deliberately design.

**The RISA Framework**
Arvin introduced the RISA Framework as a strategic approach to personal development:
- Relevance: Understanding your context and what truly matters to your goals.
- Innovation: Finding unique ways to grow and solve problems.
- Strategy: Choosing the right processes and paths.
- Adaptability: Adjusting to changes while staying true to your core purpose.

**Personal Narrative**
The talk draws on Arvin's own milestones — such as graduating in 3.5 years and earning a master's degree in the Netherlands — to illustrate how rejecting FOMO and trusting one's own timeline leads to meaningful success.

**Call to action**
The talk concludes by asking the audience: "What kind of product do you want to become?"
