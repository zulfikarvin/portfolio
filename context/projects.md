## Champio (in development)
A learning-and-diagnostics platform for Indonesian university students competing in business case, business plan, and academic essay competitions.
It combines two modules:
1. Track-based learning — ordered modules per competition format, gated by quiz milestones, plus a library of winning reference papers.
2. An AI diagnostic engine — teams upload a draft and receive rubric-aligned, scored feedback with evidence citations, tracking score improvements across versions.
Built with: Next.js, Supabase, Google Gemini.
Status: currently in development.
Origin: grew out of Arvin's own competition experience and the Sieca FRI competitions academy he founded.

## RoamIo (2025)
An AI travel companion for digital nomads, helping them handle visa-related administrative tasks using information that is currently scattered across the internet and rarely personalised.
Users share their situation (nationality, income, destination) and RoamIo generates a personalised plan — turning what used to take 3 days of research into 3 minutes.
Built with: Google Antigravity, Claude, ChatGPT.

## DiamondInsights (2025) — the MarketResponse project
An AI-powered market intelligence platform built for MarketResponse, a research company whose clients were relying on external AI tools to interpret static research reports — quietly costing the company control over its own client relationships.
DiamondInsights maximises the value of those reports through three core features:
1. Conversational chat over research documents, with chain-of-thought citations for verifiable, exportable insights.
2. Personalised cross-selling suggestions based on usage patterns.
3. A "What-If Engine" that turns historical data into a dynamic, predictive reflection of the market.
Result: placed 4th best project in the Artificial Intelligence course of the MSc programme.
Built with: Google Antigravity, ChatGPT, Claude.
Links: GitHub https://github.com/zulfikarvin/DiamondInsights_Marketresponse.git · Prototype https://canva.link/l74neg58izwc24m
Context: this is the project sometimes referred to as "the AI-based solution for MarketResponse". DiamondInsights and the MarketResponse case are the same piece of work.

## Buddy Abroad (2025)
A mobile app helping international students navigate settling into a new country — from MVV and student visas to housing, transportation cards, and cultural norms — information that is currently fragmented across too many sources.
Two features:
1. A Bureaucratic tab with step-by-step, personalised guides and reminders for every administrative task, from applying for a visa to opening a bank account.
2. An Adaptive tab that turns cultural adaptation into a gamified experience, using interactive scenarios and missions tailored to the gap between the user's home country and their new one.
Arvin's role: designed the project and built the prototype using Figma AI.
Result: won 3rd Best Project at the VU x Deloitte Burning Business Quest.
Links: Presentation https://canva.link/xxy73vy3p1xxj6k

## OutfitKu (2023) — the Garuda Hacks project
Born from the environmental toll of fast fashion: 2.3 million tons of fashion waste were generated in Indonesia in 2021 alone, according to the Ministry of Environment and Forestry.
OutfitKu is an AI-powered personal stylist app that helps users build a wardrobe reflecting their style while staying eco-friendly, through five features: Wardrobe Management to organise and track clothing; Wardrobe Analysis surfacing colour palettes, categories, and the utility of existing items; AI-generated outfit combinations based on preference, body shape, and lifestyle; a Community Platform for sharing and swapping outfits to support a circular fashion economy; and Recommendations that encourage more mindful purchases.
Arvin's role: project manager and co-designer.
Result: won Best No-Code Hack at Garuda Hacks 4.0, one of Southeast Asia's largest hackathons.
Built with: Figma, no-code tools, rapid prototyping.
Links: Video https://drive.google.com/file/d/1IQ6DY0G8tY8CdsY1Yeb9ymvzVLqXuYPD/view · Prototype https://www.figma.com/proto/7L6zINqwJeAl2bGNk7je8F/Dream-Team---GarudaHacks
Context: this is the project sometimes referred to as "Best No Code Project — Garuda Hacks 4.0". OutfitKu and the Garuda Hacks project are the same piece of work.
